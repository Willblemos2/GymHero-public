Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 2026

Gym Hero respects your privacy. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how your data is handled.

Information We Collect

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Authentication information
  • Workout routines
  • Workout history
  • Exercise records
  • Profile information
  • Images you choose to upload
  • Technical diagnostic information (such as crash reports)

Your Data

Your workout data belongs to you.

Gym Hero stores your information only to provide the services requested by you, including synchronization across devices and social features.

Workout information is shared with other users only when you explicitly choose to share it, such as publishing activity to the feed, joining groups or sharing achievements.

How We Use Your Information

  • Authenticate your account
  • Synchronize your workouts
  • Maintain your workout history
  • Provide optional social features
  • Improve reliability and stability
  • Protect against abuse and fraud

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not share your personal information with advertisers or marketing companies.

Some data may be processed by infrastructure providers strictly required to operate Gym Hero, such as cloud hosting, storage, application distribution and error monitoring.

Security

We use industry-standard security measures including encrypted communication and authenticated access.

Your Rights

  • Access your data
  • Correct inaccurate information
  • Delete your account
  • Request deletion of your personal information

Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time.
